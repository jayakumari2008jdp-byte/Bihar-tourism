Rajgir, Bihar

About Rajgir

Rajgir is a historic town in Bihar associated with Buddhism, Jainism and ancient Magadha.

Culture and Traditions

Rajgir has a rich spiritual heritage and hosts religious and cultural celebrations.

Traditional Clothes

Traditional Bihari sarees, dhotis and regional clothing.

Traditional Food

Litti chokha, sattu dishes and regional Bihari sweets.

Festivals

Rajgir Mahotsav and local religious celebrations.

Famous Places

Vishwa Shanti Stupa, Rajgir hills and the ancient hot springs.
