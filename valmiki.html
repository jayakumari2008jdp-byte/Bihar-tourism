Valmiki Tiger Reserve, Bihar

About Valmiki Tiger Reserve

Valmiki Tiger Reserve is located in West Champaran, Bihar, and is known for its forests and wildlife.

Culture and Traditions

The surrounding region has local traditions, village culture and community festivals.

Traditional Clothes

Traditional Bihari clothing and regional attire.

Traditional Food

Regional Bihari dishes, sattu preparations and local sweets.

Nature and Wildlife

The reserve is known for its tiger habitat, forests, birds and natural scenery.

Famous Attractions

Valmiki Tiger Reserve, Gandak River region and nearby forest landscapes.
