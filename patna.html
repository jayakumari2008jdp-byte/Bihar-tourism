Patna, Bihar

About Patna

Patna is the capital of Bihar and is known for its rich history and cultural heritage.

Culture and Traditions

Patna celebrates Chhath Puja and many other traditional festivals.

Traditional Clothes

Traditional Bihari sarees, Bhagalpuri silk and regional clothing.

Traditional Food

Litti chokha, khaja and thekua are popular traditional foods.

Famous Places

Golghar, Patna Sahib and Bihar Museum.
