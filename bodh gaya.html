Bodh Gaya, Bihar

About Bodh Gaya

Bodh Gaya is a famous Buddhist pilgrimage site where Gautama Buddha attained enlightenment.

Culture and Traditions

Bodh Gaya is known for Buddhist traditions, meditation and religious ceremonies.

Traditional Clothes

Traditional Bihari clothing and Buddhist monks' robes can be seen here.

Traditional Food

Litti chokha, sattu dishes and traditional Bihari sweets.

Festivals

Buddha Jayanti and other Buddhist celebrations.

Famous Places

Mahabodhi Temple and the Great Buddha Statue.
