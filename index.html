About Bihar

Welcome to Bihar Tourism! Bihar is one of the oldest and most culturally rich states in India. It is famous for its ancient history, religious places, historical monuments, and beautiful traditions. Bihar is the birthplace of Buddhism and Jainism and is home to world-famous destinations such as Bodh Gaya, Nalanda University, Rajgir, and Vaishali. Our mission is to help visitors explore the beauty, culture, heritage, and delicious traditional food of Bihar. Discover Bihar with us and experience its incredible history and hospitality. Bihar is one of the oldest and most culturally rich states in India.

Bihar Tourism Culture

Explore the traditional food, clothes, festivals and culture of Bihar.

Bodh Gaya

Culture: Buddhist culture and traditions.

Traditional Clothes: Dhoti, saree and Buddhist robes.

Food: Litti Chokha, Khaja and Thekua.

Festivals: Buddha Purnima.

Nalanda

Culture: Ancient educational and Buddhist heritage.

Traditional Clothes: Dhoti, kurta and saree.

Food: Litti Chokha and Sattu Paratha.

Festivals: Chhath Puja and Buddha Purnima.

Patna

Culture: Rich historical and traditional culture.

Traditional Clothes: Dhoti-kurta and saree.

Food: Litti Chokha, Khaja and Malpua.

Festivals: Chhath Puja, Holi and Diwali.

Vaishali

Culture: Ancient Buddhist and Jain heritage.

Traditional Clothes: Dhoti, kurta and saree.

Food: Sattu, Litti Chokha and Thekua.

Festivals: Chhath Puja and Buddha Purnima.

Rajgir

Culture: Buddhist, Jain and historical traditions.

Traditional Clothes: Dhoti-kurta and saree.

Food: Litti Chokha and local Bihari dishes.

Festivals: Rajgir Mahotsav and Chhath Puja.

Valmiki Tiger Reserve

Culture: Local and indigenous community traditions.

Traditional Clothes: Traditional regional clothing.

Food: Rice, dal and local dishes.

Festivals: Chhath Puja and local festivals.
