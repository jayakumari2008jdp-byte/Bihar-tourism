Vaishali, Bihar

About Vaishali

Vaishali is an ancient historical place in Bihar associated with Lord Buddha and Lord Mahavira.

Culture and Traditions

Vaishali has a rich cultural heritage and celebrates traditional festivals.

Traditional Clothes

Traditional Bihari sarees, regional clothing and handloom products.

Traditional Food

Litti chokha, sattu-based dishes and traditional Bihari sweets.

Famous Places

Ashokan Pillar, Vishwa Shanti Stupa and ancient historical sites.
